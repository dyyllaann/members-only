# Left Sidebar Navigation
## Problem Statement
#### What user problems are we solving?
The current 'Tags' stack on the left sidebar appears flimsy and incomplete. Users desire more ways to view content (example: Home for subscribed content and Explore for new content). Users need a persistent, predictable way to move between core areas of IvyLink. Without primary navigation, discovering and returning to Home, Explore, Profile, and future areas requires direct links or browser navigation.

## Proposed Solution
#### What are we building?
Replace the 'tags' stack with a fixed left sidebar with links to Home, Explore, Notifications, Profile, and Settings. Clearly indicate the active route and visually distinguish unavailable future destinations (cursor selection, muted text, no effect on click).

## Success Metrics
#### How do we know this worked?
* Verified successful navigation between Home, Explore, and Profile.

Theoretical examples:
* Lower rate of users leaving after viewing a single feed.
* A majority of active users navigate through the sidebar during a session.
* Low rate of clicks on unavailable navigation items.

## Out of Scope
#### What are we not building right now?
* Notification counts and real notification delivery.
* Full Settings and Notifications pages.
* Click/hover counter to measure interest in unavailable features.
* Minimize the left sidebar and show only icons on smaller screens.
